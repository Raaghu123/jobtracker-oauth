JobTracker Sync

A private, single-user automation tool.

JobTracker Sync mirrors the owner's local spreadsheets to the owner's own Google Drive as native Google Sheets, and appends rows to a personal lead-tracking sheet. It runs only for its single owner's account and is not offered as a service to anyone else.

It requests a single Google scope, drive.file, which allows access only to the specific files the tool itself creates and the sheets its owner explicitly shares with it.
