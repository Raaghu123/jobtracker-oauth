Privacy Policy

JobTracker Sync · Last updated: 18 October 2026

JobTracker Sync is a private automation tool operated by and for a single individual (the "owner"). This policy describes what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

The tool requests one Google OAuth scope: drive.file. This grants access only to files that the tool itself creates, and to files the owner explicitly authorizes. It cannot read, list, or modify any other file in the owner's Google Drive.

What it does with the data

It uploads the owner's own spreadsheet files to the owner's own Google Drive (converting them to Google Sheets), and appends rows to a spreadsheet the owner maintains. All processing happens on the owner's own computer and Google account.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party. There is no analytics, no tracking, and no server operated by the tool owner that receives Google data. OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's machine.

Data retention and deletion

Data lives in the owner's Google Drive under the owner's control. The owner can delete it at any time from Google Drive, and can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
